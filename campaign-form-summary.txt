FROM SEARCH TO SEND
The campaign form, in six steps

The campaign form takes you from an empty screen to a set of
personalised, approved emails in six steps. Each step is a decision
point: nothing moves forward on its own.


--------------------------------------------------------------------
THE SIX STEPS
--------------------------------------------------------------------

01  CAMPAIGN SETUP

    Name, language of the emails, and how many companies to put
    through the search. You pick the sender address from the
    mailboxes that actually exist in the sending account, add the
    seller's website and up to two reference customers, and write
    any guidance for the texts.

    Roughly seven out of ten companies yield a usable contact, and
    the form says so next to the field - so 14 companies for 10
    contacts.


02  SELLING POINTS AND TARGET CUSTOMER

    The AI reads the seller's website and drafts both the selling
    points and the profile of the customer to approach. Both are
    editable text - you correct anything that is off before moving
    on.

    The form keeps sender and recipient strictly apart, so the
    profile describes the companies you want to reach, not the
    company you are writing on behalf of.


03  COMPANY FILTERS

    City or postal code with a radius around it, plus the services
    the companies should offer. You also choose here whether to work
    from the companies already in the database or collect fresh ones
    for this campaign.

    While you type a city, a live counter shows how many companies
    the database already holds for that area - so you can see before
    searching whether a fresh collection is needed.


04  SEARCH RESULT

    The companies found, each with name, city and postal code, and
    website. The page shows how many companies you asked for against
    how many were actually taken over, what the lookup will cost,
    and how many credits remain.

    Contacts are looked up in one bundled request instead of one per
    company, and every address is checked by a paid verification
    service before it can be used.


05  DISPATCH SETTINGS

    Sending mailbox, daily limit, the hours and weekdays mail may go
    out, and the signature. One switch decides the whole character
    of the campaign: PREVIEW sends only to your own test addresses,
    LIVE sends to the real recipients.


06  WRITE AND RELEASE

    The AI writes one personalised email per contact. A quality
    check reads each text back and flags any claim it cannot support
    from the source material. You read every email in full, send any
    of them to yourself first to see exactly how it will land, and
    confirm them one by one. The whole set exports to Excel.


--------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THE TOOL WILL NOT DO ON ITS OWN
--------------------------------------------------------------------

  - No email leaves without a person confirming that specific email.

  - Handing a campaign over to the sending engine creates it PAUSED.
    Handover is not a send.

  - In preview mode the tool refuses any address that belongs to a
    real recipient - a preview only ever goes to your own test
    address.

  - Emails flagged by the quality check stay behind until a person
    either clears them or has the text rewritten.

  THE FINAL GATE: no campaign is activated and no mail is sent
  without Dafina saying so explicitly, each time, for every campaign
  and every tool.
